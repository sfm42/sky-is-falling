The Doom-o-Meter: Test Any Post

Results appear here. No data leaves your browser.
How it works. Every hour, a robot reads a sample of public posts and headlines that contain doom words, rates how dramatic each one sounds, and averages the loudest half. A second robot does the same for AI chatter to set the Robot Uprising Risk. Each needle blends two things: how many doom posts are appearing compared with a normal hour, and how dramatic they sound. It measures tone, not topic: a calm report about something serious scores low. No accounts are named, tracked or stored, only the overall numbers.
Disclaimer. For entertainment only. Not a forecast of anything. If the needle is in the red, please drink some water and go outside.
